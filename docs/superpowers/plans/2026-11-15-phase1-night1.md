# Phase 1, Night 1 "The House Learns You": Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace Act 1 with the v2 Night 1: a free routine full of choices (dinner, Cooper, chores, TV, the house's jokes, the phone), a habit log that records it, and dread paced by a tension director after a settled-in gate.

**Architecture:** Five new pure UMD modules in `js/engine/` (habits, tension, scarePool, food, tvSchedule), written test-first and driven by data files in `content/`. Act 1 becomes a thin composer over small routine modules in `js/night1/`, each returning `{ items, update(dt), dispose() }`. The phone gains apps (camera, pet cam, photos, Flappy Cooper, mixtape); the camera, pet cam and bathroom mirror render the existing scene into small targets.

**Tech Stack:** Three.js r128 (bundled), Web Audio synthesis, Node 20 `node --test`, classic scripts opened from `file://`.

**Spec:** `docs/story-v2.md` (Night 1 section, scare pool, new systems, verification) and `docs/design-v2.md` (research rules, per-phase checks). Handoff: vault `output/handoffs/2026-11-16-oakridge-crawlspace-v2.md`.

## Global Constraints

- Opens by double-clicking `index.html`: classic `<script>` tags, no ES modules, no `fetch`. Pure modules use the UMD wrapper already in `js/engine/*.js`.
- No downloaded assets in this phase. All art is code-built or canvas-drawn; all sound is synthesized. Ask Rohan before any model-kit download (none planned here).
- All text hand-written, in `content/text.js` (or `content/tv.js` for captions), checked by each act's `requiredText`.
- At most 3 real jump scares in the episode; Night 1 has none. Every scare beat gets a relief beat.
- Cooper is never hurt. No weapons.
- Night 1 runs 7:30 PM to 2:15 AM: `clockDef { startMin: 1170, endMin: 1575, durationSec: 960 }`.
- Settled-in gate: 4 routine tasks done or 480 s elapsed.
- Draw calls under about 150 in the busiest view (pet cam or mirror active).
- Commit locally after each task. Never push.

## Review Focus

1. **A player who skips or idles** (never photographs the hall, never goes near the laundry door, stands still for ten minutes, stays outside): Night 1 must still reach bed. Every dread beat has a timeout fallback. Pinned by the `night1Afk` sweep flow (Task 12).
2. **Pointer lock lost inside a modal** (Esc while the microwave keypad, a choice or the camera is up): resuming must leave the controller unfrozen and the modal usable or closed. Pinned by the `modalUnlock` sweep flow (Task 12).
3. **Restarting the chapter mid-night**: the laundry door, TV, Cooper's pose and every audio loop reset; no doubled hum. Pinned by the `restart` sweep flow (Task 12).
4. **A beat firing while the player is outdoors or in the south wing**: the travelling scratching waits for the player to be indoors; Dave's window waits until the player is out of the south wing (existing rule). Covered by the AFK flow standing outdoors for part of its run.
5. **A huge frame delta** (tab hidden, then back): the tension director clamps `dt` to 0.1 s like the clock. Pinned by a unit test (Task 2).

### Task 2: `tension.js`, the Director

**Files:** Create `js/engine/tension.js`, `tests/tension.test.js`.

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `createTension(opts)` with defaults `{ rise: 0.012, threshold: 0.6, cooldown: 20, reliefSec: 30, idleLimit: 40, budget: Infinity }` → `{ update(dt, signs) → null | {kind:'ambient'} | {kind:'idle'}, peak(fear), relieve(sec), ready(), spend(), menace(), stress(), inRelief(), budgetLeft() }`; `stressFrom(signs)` → 0..1. `signs = { turnRate, moving, backingUp, sprinting, lightOn }`.
- Rules: `dt` clamped to 0.1. Stress eases toward `stressFrom(signs)` at `dt*2`. Menace rises `rise*dt*(1 - 0.7*stress)`, decays 0.05/s in relief. Ambient fires when menace ≥ threshold, cooldown ≤ 0, not in relief, stress ≤ 0.6, budget > 0; then menace −= 0.35, cooldown = `cooldown`, budget −= 1. Idle (`!moving && turnRate < 0.3`) for `idleLimit` s with cooldown ≤ 0 and not in relief fires `idle` and starts a relief of `reliefSec/2`. `peak(f)`: f ≥ 5 → menace 0, relief `reliefSec`; else menace −= 0.3. `spend()` sets cooldown. `ready()` = not in relief and cooldown ≤ 0. `stressFrom`: turnRate > 2.5 → +0.4, sprinting +0.3, backingUp +0.2, lightOn +0.1, clamped.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests:** `nothing fires before menace reaches the threshold` (rise 0.1: no event in the first 5.8 s, ambient by 6.2 s); `cooldown blocks a second ambient`; `peak(6) forces relief and zeroes menace; peak(3) does not`; `ready() is false during relief and cooldown`; `high stress slows the rise and blocks firing`; `idle fires after idleLimit, moving resets the timer, and idle is followed by relief`; `budget stops events`; `update(30) advances only 0.1 s`; `stressFrom values`.
